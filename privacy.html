Privacy Policy

Evansen Co Living PMS · Last updated 19 November 2026

Evansen Co Living PMS is an internal property-management tool used only by the staff of Evansen Co Living (Ungasan, Bali) to manage guest reservations. It is not a public service.

Information we handle

Guest reservation details entered by our staff (such as name, contact details, stay dates, payments and notes) and documents our staff upload for a booking (such as passport scans or payment slips).

Use of Google account data

When our administrator connects the business Google account (evansen.coliving@gmail.com), the app uses:

  • Google Drive (only files created by this app) — to store and open documents uploaded for a reservation, in a folder named "ECL Booker Folder".
  • Gmail (send only) — to send booking confirmation emails to guests from the business account.
  • Basic account info (email address) — to show which account is connected.

The app does not read your other Drive files or your Gmail inbox. Google data is used only to provide these features, is not sold or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Reservation records are stored in our database (Supabase) and are only accessible to signed-in staff. Uploaded documents are stored in the business Google Drive. Access credentials for Google are stored on the server and are never exposed in the website.

Removing access

The administrator can disconnect the Google account at any time from the app's Settings page, or from Google Account permissions.
